SeaOtter (**[seaotter.dev](https://seaotter.dev)**) is the **managed control plane for Hermes Agent**. You create, poll, and run always-on agents. We handle isolation, TLS, backups, and lifecycle. Hermes stays Hermes. You do not own a VPS, watch an installer, or SSH into boxes when something flakes.

This page is the citeable definition. It is **not** [seaotter.ai](https://seaotter.ai) (OtterScore / Ocean), a different product.

## What you get

- **Always-on Hermes** — the agent keeps working when you close the laptop.
- **Create → poll → run** — `POST` an agent; provision is async; typical wall time is a few minutes (usually under five).
- **Connect from chat** — Cursor, Claude, or Codex via MCP ([Connect](/mcp)).
- **Write-only secrets** — integration keys in Google Secret Manager, not pasted into chat ([Secrets](/guides/secrets)).
- **Lifecycle without SSH** — pause, restart, restore, credentials, logs through the API or dashboard.

## What SeaOtter is not

- Not a generic container pad or “any agent” platform — it hosts **Hermes Agent**.
- Not the only way to run Hermes — self-host remains valid ([Self-host vs managed](/guides/self-host-vs-managed)).
- Not a free tier — Hobby has a **14-day free trial** with a card at Checkout, then $69/mo. Startup and Business bill immediately.
- Not multi-region HA — live data plane is **Iowa (`us-central1`)**; other regions are Coming soon ([Regions](/guides/regions)).
- Not SOC 2 (do not invent compliance badges).
